FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

D.I.M. Bright Ideas LLC

🔵 What kind of products do you sell?

We sell custom prints on T-shirts, Tumblers, Totes and more. Many items are made-to-order, which means each piece is unique.

🔵 Do you offer custom or personalized orders?

Yes! We offer custom and personalized items. Please double-check all personalization details before submitting your order. Once production begins, changes may not be possible. Custom items are final sale.

🔵 Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Because many of our items are handmade or customized:

  • Custom or personalized items cannot be returned or exchanged
  • Non-custom items may qualify for return if requested within 14 days of delivery
  • Items must be unused and in original condition
  • Return eligibility is determined on a case-by-case basis.

🔵 What if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
  • Include clear photos of the item and packaging
  • Approved claims will be resolved with a replacement or refund.

🔵 How long does shipping take?

Processing times vary:

  • Ready-to-ship items: 2–7 business days
  • Custom orders: 5–14 business days
  • Shipping times depend on the carrier and destination.
  • We are not responsible for delays caused by postal services or weather.

🔵 What if I entered the wrong shipping address?

Please review your address carefully before placing your order. We are not responsible for orders shipped to incorrect addresses provided by the customer.

🔵 Will my item look exactly like the photos?

Because our items are handmade:

  • Slight variations in color, size, or finish may occur
  • These variations are normal and part of the handmade process
  • Screen settings may also affect color appearance.

🔵 How does apparel sizing work?

Apparel sizing is approximate. We recommend sizing up if unsure.

🔵 What payment methods do you accept?

Accepted payment methods are displayed at checkout. Orders must be paid in full before production begins.

🔵 How is my personal information used?

We only collect information needed to:

  • Process orders
  • Communicate with customers
  • Provide support
  • We do not sell or share personal information except when required for shipping or payment processing.

🔵 What does D.I.M. stand for?

D.I.M. stands for Did It Myself. It represents creativity, independence, and the joy of bringing ideas to life. At D.I.M. Bright Ideas, D.I.M. reflects our hands-on approach — every design is thoughtfully created, customized, and produced with care.
