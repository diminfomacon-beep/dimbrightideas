Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for returns or refunds:

  • Custom or personalized products
  • Seasonal or holiday items
  • Clearance or final sale items

Returns (Non-Custom Items Only)

If a non-custom item qualifies for return:

  • Requests must be made within 14 days of delivery
  • Items must be unused and in original condition
  • Buyer is responsible for return shipping

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your item arrives damaged or incorrect:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
  • Include clear photos of the item and packaging

Approved issues will be resolved through replacement or refund.

In accordance with Georgia law, refunds and returns are subject to the policies clearly disclosed at the time of purchase.

By completing a purchase, customers acknowledge and agree to the return and refund terms stated on this website.

Custom and personalized items are exempt from return as permitted by Georgia consumer protection guidelines.
